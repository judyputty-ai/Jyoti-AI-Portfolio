Skills

  • 🔍

    User Research

    Uncovering goals, pain points, and context through interviews, surveys, and synthesis so decisions stay grounded in real people.

  • 📐

    Wireframing

    Sketching layouts and flows in low fidelity to nail structure, hierarchy, and screen-to-screen logic before visual polish.

  • 🧩

    Prototyping

    Building clickable models to test interactions, gather feedback, and iterate quickly on how a product should feel in use.

  • 🎨

    Figma

    Using components, auto layout, and shared libraries so designs scale, stay consistent, and hand off cleanly to development.

  • ✅

    Usability Testing

    Running moderated sessions and task-based tests to spot friction, validate flows, and prioritize improvements with clear evidence.

  • ✨

    Visual Design

    Refining typography, color, spacing, and imagery so interfaces feel cohesive, accessible, and aligned with the brand story.
