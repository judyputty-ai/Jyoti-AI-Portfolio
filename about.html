About

JP

Hey there! I'm Jyoti Putty, a UX designer with about five years under my belt. Before diving into design, I studied psychology and spent some time in customer support, which really shaped how I think about user experience. I believe good UX isn't about flashy visuals—it's about making things clear, respecting people's time, and creating inclusive experiences that work for everyone.

I love collaborating closely with research and engineering teams, getting honest feedback, and building those 'ugly early' prototypes that help us learn fast. What gets me excited is seeing someone stuck on a flow, and then with a small tweak—like simpler wording or a better default—watching them breeze through with confidence. Whether it's signing up for a service, navigating a dashboard, or using an app on the go, I focus on those real outcomes: less hassle, fewer mistakes, and experiences that just feel right.
